# Decision Journal: Clarity Support

Decision Journal is a local-first iPhone app for recording decisions, forecasts, and outcome reviews.

## Common questions

### Where is my journal stored?

New users start with on-device storage. Pro Lifetime users can enable iCloud sync in Settings. Turning sync off keeps future changes on the device but does not delete an existing private iCloud copy.

### How do I restore Pro Lifetime?

Open Settings, select Decision Journal Pro, and tap **Restore purchases**. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

### Why am I not receiving review reminders?

Open Settings in Decision Journal and confirm Review reminders is enabled. If notification access was denied, use **Open Notification Settings** and allow notifications for Decision Journal.

### How do I back up or move my data?

Use Export Journal to create Markdown, CSV, or PDF files. These exported files are not encrypted by the app, so store them somewhere you trust.

### How do I delete iCloud data?

You can remove Decision Journal data from your Apple Account’s iCloud storage settings. Deleting the app from one device alone may not remove an existing iCloud copy.

## Contact

For help, contact [decisionjournal.support@gmail.com](mailto:decisionjournal.support@gmail.com) and include your iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Do not include private journal content unless you intentionally choose to share it.
